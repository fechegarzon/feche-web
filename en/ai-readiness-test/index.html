01 / 10 · Processes

Have you identified the process that consumes the most repetitive work hours?

What the test measures

  • Processes: Write down, on one page, the process that takes the most time: steps, who does it and how long it takes. Without that, no agent has anywhere to land.
  • Data: Put the data for the process you want to improve in one place (even a well-kept sheet) and define a success metric before building.
  • Channels: Move WhatsApp customer contact to a company number with logged conversations. It's where an agent pays off fastest in Latin America.
  • Team: Name a project owner with real time for it and define who approves what the agent proposes. AI without an owner stays a pilot.
  • Governance & risk: Before launching, define what the agent may do alone, what a person must approve and how customer data is protected.
