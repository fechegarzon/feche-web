How it's calculated

  1. 01Hours freed = people × weekly hours of repetitive work × 4.33 weeks × the share the agent absorbs.
  2. 02Value of those hours = hours freed × hourly cost (monthly cost ÷ 173 working hours).
  3. 03Net savings = value of hours freed − monthly cost to run the agent. Initial build cost is not included.

This is an indicative estimate: real return depends on the process, data quality and how much of the work is truly repetitive. Hours freed don't always mean lower payroll; often they turn into more sales or better service with the same team.

FAQ

Which processes usually have the best return?
High-volume processes with clear rules: answering and qualifying WhatsApp leads, document review, case follow-up and operational reporting.
How much does it cost to build the agent?
It depends on integrations, channel and business rules. We estimate it during the diagnostic, together with the running cost, before building anything.
