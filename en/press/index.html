Ready-to-use bios

Federico Garzón Rodríguez builds and runs production AI agents for financial and real estate companies in Latin America. He is Head of AI & Growth at LQN Hipotecas, a mortgage platform backed by Pegasus, Angel Ventures and 500 Global. Before that he co-founded and ran DORA, the fintech that replaced the co-signer in Colombian leases: 2,000 tenants, USD 8.4 million in annualized guaranteed rent and a 0.8% default rate. DORA was acquired.
